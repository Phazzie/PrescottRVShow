# It Ain't No Mirage (draft 1)

**Style:** front-porch bluegrass, banjo, fiddle, washboard, upright bass, foot stomps, gravelly old-timer male lead, group harmony chorus, tongue-in-cheek; opens with a lone dry fiddle drone, builds, full stop, then explodes into a fast stomping chorus with gang vocals

**Hook (the catchy part):** call-and-response chant: "It ain't no mi-RAGE! (It ain't no mirage!) / It's DJ, the RV Guy!" Short, shoutable, and it names him and the twist at the same time.

```
[Intro - lone fiddle drone, slow, dry, no drums]
Dead car, dirt road, way out off 69

[Build - banjo roll creeps in, getting faster]
No bars on the phone, and the water's all gone
Buzzard on the roof, and he ain't movin' on

[Break - full band stop, one hard hit]

[Hushed, half-time]
Somethin' shinin' way out where the road meets the sky...

[Chorus - full band explodes, fast stomp, gang vocals]
It ain't no mirage! (It ain't no mirage!)
It's DJ, the RV Guy!
Cold drinks, new keys, and a whole new ride
It ain't no mirage! It's DJ, the RV Guy!

[Tag - spoken-sung, then one final hit]
Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!

[Verse 2 - fast]
Left the old sedan with the buzzard on the hood
DJ said he'd keep it, and the buzzard said "good"
Rollin' into sunset in a house with wheels
Kids in the back askin' "Daddy, is it real?"

[Chorus - full band, gang vocals]
It ain't no mirage! (It ain't no mirage!)
It's DJ, the RV Guy!
Cold drinks, new keys, and a whole new ride
It ain't no mirage! It's DJ, the RV Guy!

[Outro - gang vocals, final stomp]
Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!
It ain't no mirage!
```
