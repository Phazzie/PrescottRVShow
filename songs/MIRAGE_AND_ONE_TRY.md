# DJ the RV Guy: campaign songs for 30-second videos

Both are front-porch bluegrass, the "Black Tank" sound, and both are built so the first ~30 seconds tell the whole story. Name rule: always "DJ the RV Guy", never just "DJ" or "a DJ".

---

## Song 1: "Ain't No Mirage" (for the video thread's "The Mirage" concept)

**Suno style prompt:** front-porch bluegrass, banjo, fiddle, washboard, upright bass, foot stomps, gravelly old-timer male lead, dry straight-faced deadpan delivery, opens with a lone dry fiddle drone, builds, full stop, then explodes into a fast stomping chorus with shouted gang vocals, catchy chant hook

**How it maps to the video clock:**

| Video | Song section |
|---|---|
| 0–5 s grabber (dead car, heat) | Intro: lone dry fiddle + "Dead car, dirt road, way off 69" |
| 5–12 s conflict builds | Build: banjo creeps in, no signal, no water, buzzard |
| 12 s shimmer | Break: full stop, one hard hit |
| 12–18 s disbelief walk | Hushed: "Is that a row of RVs wavin' in the heat?" |
| 18 s "it's real" | Chorus hits: "Ain't no mirage! (Ain't no lie!)" |
| 18–25 s DJ, cooler, keys | "It's DJ the RV Guy! / Cooler full of cold drinks and the keys to ride" |
| 25–30 s drive off, end card | Tag: "Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!" + final hit |

Verse 2 and the last chorus are for a longer cut (DJ keeps the sedan).

## Song 2: "One Try" (The Driveway Show)

**Story:** neighbors in lawn chairs watch a couple try to back a 40-ft fifth wheel into a 30-ft driveway → DJ the RV Guy puts them in one that fits their drive → it backs in on the first try and the neighbors fold their chairs.

**Suno style prompt:** front-porch bluegrass, banjo, fiddle, washboard, upright bass, foot stomps, gravelly old-timer male lead, dry straight-faced deadpan delivery, fast, shouted gang-vocal chant chorus, catchy chant hook

```
[Intro - gang vocal shout, then banjo kicks in]
They're backin' it in again!

[Verse 1 - fast]
Forty feet of fifth wheel, thirty feet of drive
Whole street's out in lawn chairs like the Fourth of July
Mailbox is nervous and the juniper's thin
Pass the popcorn, neighbor, let the show begin

[Chorus - full band, gang vocals]
One try! (One try!) DJ the RV Guy!
Fits the drive, backs right in, and the neighbors sigh
One try! (One try!) DJ the RV Guy!

[Tag]
Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!

[Verse 2 - fast]
Twenty-six feet of trailer, thirty feet of drive
Backed it in one pass and the new mailbox survived
Neighbors folded up the lawn chairs, took the popcorn back inside
Now the RV show's in town, out on 69

[Chorus]

[Outro - gang vocals, final stomp]
Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!
One try!
```

### Review log
- **Fresh reviewer:** hook passes ("try/Guy" rhymes, chantable). The 30 s cut had no resolution, since it only lived in verse 2 → chorus line 2 now carries it and rhymes with the hook (taken, reworded). "Mailbox is a goner" made the couple the joke → "Mailbox is nervous" (taken). Verse 1 repeated the intro line → "Pass the popcorn, neighbor, let the show begin" (taken). "Only RV show in town" is an unsupported superlative → dropped (taken). "Here till Sunday" vs "through the 18th": kept "here till Sunday" because it's DJ's own sign-off; the end card shows the date.
