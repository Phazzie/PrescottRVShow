# One Try (The Driveway Show), draft 1

**Story (30 s):** neighbors in lawn chairs watch a couple jam a 40-ft fifth wheel into a 30-ft driveway (problem) → DJ the RV Guy puts them in one that fits their drive → they back the new one in on the first try and the neighbors fold their chairs (resolution).

**Style:** front-porch bluegrass, banjo, fiddle, washboard, upright bass, foot stomps, gravelly old-timer male lead, dry straight-faced deadpan delivery, fast, shouted gang-vocal chant chorus, catchy chant hook

**Hook:** "One try! (One try!) / DJ the RV Guy!" — try/Guy rhyme, chantable, names him.

```
[Intro - gang vocal shout, then banjo kicks in]
They're backin' it in again!

[Verse 1 - fast]
Forty feet of fifth wheel, thirty feet of drive
Whole street's out in lawn chairs like the Fourth of July
Mailbox is a goner and the juniper's thin
Somebody's sellin' popcorn, they're backin' it in again

[Chorus - full band, gang vocals]
One try! (One try!) DJ the RV Guy!
He'll put you in a rig that fits your drive
One try! (One try!) DJ the RV Guy!

[Tag]
Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!

[Verse 2 - fast]
Twenty-six feet of trailer, thirty feet of drive
Backed it in one pass and the new mailbox survived
Neighbors folded up the lawn chairs, took the popcorn back inside
Now the only RV show in town is out on 69

[Chorus - full band, gang vocals]
One try! (One try!) DJ the RV Guy!
He'll put you in a rig that fits your drive
One try! (One try!) DJ the RV Guy!

[Outro - gang vocals, final stomp]
Prescott Valley, out on 69, here till Sunday!
One try!
```
