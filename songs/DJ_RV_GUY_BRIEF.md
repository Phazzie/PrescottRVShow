## Name rule
Always **"DJ the RV Guy"**: in supers, end card, captions. Never "DJ" alone and never "a DJ" (it reads as a disc jockey). It's his own brand, said on camera and on his flyers.

## Risks and must-dos
- **DJ's face** is the top risk (the pipeline's live-action faces drift). Medium/close shots of DJ get the identity check against I8yL; fallback is real footage of him or a face-swap pass.
- **His outfit:** at Prescott he does *not* wear the yellow Pete's RV shirt (Trizzy confirmed: different shirt). Ask Trizzy what he wears there before generating him. In the old Richmond footage, blur or avoid the Pete's RV logo.
- **I8yL photo:** crop the Spider-Man art out completely; never reproduce Spider-Man.
- **"He keeps the wreck"** implies DJ takes trade-ins. Confirm with DJ before it goes public. (It fits his other brand, DJ's Auto Liquidators.)
- No prices, financing, other dealers' brands or the show's logo. All text added in the edit.
- Nothing about temperatures (it's October in Prescott); let the shimmer and dust say "heat".
